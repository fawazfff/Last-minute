How it works

Too much work. Not enough time. Here is what LastMinute does.

You give LastMinute the truth about your deadline and your workload. It helps you build a smaller plan that can actually fit.

01

Tell it what “done” means.

Start with the thing you need to finish. For example: “Submit my school project” or “Finish my hackathon app.”

02

Give it the real deadline.

Use the actual deadline, not the time you hope to finish. LastMinute needs the truth to make a useful plan.

03

Say how much time you can really work.

If there are 6 hours until the deadline but you can only work for 4 hours, enter 4 hours. Sleeping, eating, classes and meetings are not work time.

04

Dump all your tasks.

Add everything you think you need to do and how long each task may take. You do not need to put them in order first.

05

LastMinute checks the math.

If your tasks need 8 hours but you only have 5, it tells you the plan does not fit. It also keeps a small buffer because real life is rarely perfect.

06

Decide what survives.

MUST means do it. REDUCE means make it smaller. DEFER means do it later. KILL means drop it for this deadline.

07

Follow one next action.

When the plan fits, LastMinute gives you the next task and a timer. You do not need to keep staring at the whole list.

08

If a task takes longer, check the plan again.

Tell LastMinute you need more time. It can help you move or cut less important work so the deadline stays protected.

MUST

Without this, the main goal may fail.

REDUCE

Keep the useful part, but make it smaller.

DEFER

Useful, but it can wait until after the deadline.

KILL

Stop spending deadline time on it.

The rule

Reality first.

LastMinute will not pretend 8 hours fits inside 5. A smaller plan you can finish is better than a perfect plan you cannot.
